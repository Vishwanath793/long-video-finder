Long Video Finder

Zee Business First Trade and CNBC Awaaz: find long streams, save their subtitles, and search what was said.

Channels and search queries

Use {date} in the query, e.g. "CNBC Awaaz {date}". "Channel contains" filters results by uploader name.

Add videos by URL

Paste YouTube links, one per line (watch, youtu.be or live links all work). Their subtitles go into your Library like the rest, whatever their length. Videos added here are never replaced by the automatic search.

Leave Date empty to use the date in the video's title, else its YouTube publish date.

Checking your YouTube sign-in…

One time only: a separate Chrome window opens. Sign in with the account that has the channel membership, then close that window. After that you only paste video URLs. The sign-in stays in this app's own browser profile on this PC (data\youtube-browser) and is separate from your normal Chrome.

Improve accuracy

      
Counts skip ads, jingles, show promos and sponsor credits (caption lines that air word-for-word on 3+ days), and a name
        repeated within 10 seconds counts once. Captions misspell names and the watchlist can't know every stock, so these two
        helpers suggest additions from your transcripts. Check the example line before you click Add: some sound-alikes are other words.

      

        
        
        
      

      

      

    

    

      

        Edit watchlist (stocks and their spellings)
        
Captions mix English and Hindi, so give each stock both spellings, e.g. "reliance" and "रिलायंस". Longer names win, so "tech mahindra" is not counted as "mahindra".

    Automatic daily run
    
        
    Every weekday at the set time, Windows runs the collector. It skips NSE holidays, saves that day's subtitles, and retries any of the last 10 days that are missing, so a day the PC was off still gets collected later. The PC must be on and logged in at that time.
